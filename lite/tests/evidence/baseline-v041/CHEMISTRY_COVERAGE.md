# Chemistry Master Library v1 / Lite v0.4.0

117 frozen free/deprotected scaffolds plus PO/PS/PS2/Me-P/PN(DMI)/MsPA and an implicit PMO phosphorodiamidate link. REGRESSION_VALIDATED means implemented atomic constitution, valence/formula, stereo-export and layout regression checks; it does not mean experimental validation of every possible oligo.

Implemented families: DNA, RNA, 2′-OMe, 2′-F, MOE, LNA, FANA, UNA, NMA, ENA, S-GNA A/C/G/T, C16/C22 2-O-alkyl, TNA, PMO, Benner Z, 5-(E)-VP + OMe/MOE, amino C3/C6, C3 spacer, PEG3–7, 6-FAM, L96, NAG37. Sugar family operations cover more than the original example tokens. PMO and TNA currently require pure chains. PMO uses uncapped 5-OH / 3-NH ends, not a guessed commercial end cap. GNA/TNA label their native 3→2 attachment direction.

FAM is fixed to the 6-FAM carboxamido-C6-phosphoramidite-derived variant, neutral lactone representation, with the reagent P and two O-pivaloyl groups removed. NAG37 is 5-terminal and L96 is 3-terminal. L96 loses DMT, nine O-acetyl groups and support succinate; NAG37 loses phosphoramidite auxiliaries and nine O-acetyl groups. Retain GalNAc N-acetyl groups. The assembled phosphate belongs to the linkage, not the free scaffold. Full transformations and original formulas are in CONJUGATE_TRANSFORMATIONS.json.

NAG37's PubChem record omits the cis-1,4 ring configuration. It is added explicitly from the cis-cyclohexyl identity in the supplier's systematic definition and stored as relative_stereo with its source. The two same-face external wedges are retained in MOL/CDXML. The public reagent is never treated as the final molecular-weight contribution.

Pending entries return STRUCTURE_VARIANT_PENDING and no graph/mass. cOct returns PENDING_CHEMISTRY. Custom MOL/SDF can complete pending special-unit IDs. No unique dye/linker variant is inferred from a short name.
