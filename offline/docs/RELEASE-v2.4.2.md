# Oligo Toolkit v2.4.2

## What's new

- Batch Post-functionalization now uses one Excel row per final molecule. Fill in Sequence and Step 1, Step 2, and onward. The step menus draw from built-in reagents, your User Library, uploaded MOL/SDF, and disulfide reduction. Steps run left to right through the shared Molecule Builder chemistry, and ambiguous products can be reviewed in Toolkit.
- Batch results report chemistry, structure export, and OD independently, so an unavailable format or OD estimate does not obscure a successful reaction.
- Presentation C uses an updated PMO schematic, and Chinese and English labels are more consistent across the interface.
- Branch elongation attaches the child through its 3′ end. ChemDraw CDXML exports use a smaller coordinate scale. Thiol coupling with methylsulfonyl-1,3,4-oxadiazole is available in Quick and Advanced modes, and the v2.4.1 JQ1 SPAAC fixes are retained.

Download `Oligo-Toolkit-v2.4.2.zip`, extract it to a short path such as `C:\Oligo`, and open `Start.html` in Edge or Chrome.
